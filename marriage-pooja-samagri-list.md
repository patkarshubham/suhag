# Complete Marriage Pooja Samagri & Ritual Items List

A master product-catalog list for a marriage/wedding pooja samagri e-commerce store — covering pre-wedding, wedding-day, and havan/ritual essentials.

---

## 1. Core Wedding Ritual Items

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Kalash (Sacred Pot) | Filled with water, mango leaves & coconut on top; placed near mandap to invite devtas |
| Mangalsutra | Tied by groom around bride's neck; marks completion of marriage |
| Sindoor (Vermilion) | Applied in bride's maang during kanyadaan/saat phere; sign of marriage |
| Haldi (Turmeric) | Used in haldi ceremony for shuddhi (cleansing) before rituals |
| Garlands (Varmala) | Exchanged by bride and groom |
| Sacred Thread (Mauli/Kalava) | Tied on hands during sankalp for protection |
| Puffed Rice (Lava) | Offered into the fire during phere |

## 2. General Offering Items

- Coconut (whole, dry)
- Flowers (loose & garlands)
- Betel leaves (paan)
- Betel nuts (supari)
- Rice (akshat)
- Kumkum
- Chandan (sandalwood paste/powder)
- Turmeric powder
- Abir/Gulal

## 3. Havan (Fire Ritual) Essentials

- Havan Kund (fire pit)
- Havan Samidha (dry wood sticks)
- Ghee (pure, for havan use)
- Rice and sugar (for ahuti)
- Dry coconut (for ahuti)
- Panchamrit (milk, curd, ghee, honey, sugar)
- Camphor (Bhimseni/regular)
- Incense sticks / Dhoop sticks
- Navdhanyam (nine sacred grains)
- Gangajal (holy water for purification)

## 4. Lighting & Diya Items

- Small brass diya (reusable)
- Pure cow ghee diyas (ready-to-light)
- Cotton wicks (batti)
- Matchbox/lighter
- Deepams / oil lamps (for mandap decor)

## 5. Thali & Organization Items

- Pooja thali set (with compartments)
- Small steel/brass bowls (katori) for individual items
- Clean cloth (for covering/wiping)
- Aarti thali with bell

### Suggested Bundling Idea for Your Store
You could package these as **ceremony-wise combo kits** (e.g., "Haldi Ceremony Kit," "Havan Essentials Kit," "Complete Wedding Pooja Kit") rather than selling only individual SKUs — this tends to perform well for wedding shoppers who prefer one-stop bundled purchases.
